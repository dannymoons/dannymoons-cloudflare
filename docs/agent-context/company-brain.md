## 6. Core Personal Stance on Sustainable Web

See `/home/danny/vault/wiki/concepts/danny-personal-sustainable-web-development-stance.md` for the full position.

Short version:
- Sustainable web development means reducing environmental impact by building less wastefully, choosing responsible infrastructure, optimising by default, questioning unnecessary complexity, and being transparent about impact.
- It is not about making bloated websites slightly greener. It is about questioning the bloat in the first place.
- The goal is intentionality: build what is needed, build it well, avoid avoidable waste.

## 8. Claims Policy

Allowed claims:
- Danny builds websites with sustainable practices
- Danny founded Moonsio, Carbonfooter, Pixel To Planet
- Projects have achieved measurable improvements (with client approval for specifics)
- Danny has spoken at [conference/event]

Claims that need evidence or approval:
- Specific carbon or energy savings figures
- "Leading" or "expert" authority claims
- Client testimonials or outcome numbers without client approval

Claims to avoid:
- Guarantees of outcomes for future projects
- Negative claims about named individuals or companies
- Medical or health claims (those belong to Duizeligheid Nederland)
- Exaggerated sustainability impact
