# Article Brief — Article 1

## Working title
Sustainable Software Is an Engineering Quality (working title — open to better ones)

## Article type
Opinion essay / positioning piece

## Primary audience
Developers, tech leads, founders, agency owners who hear "sustainable software" and think it's about green hosting or carbon offsets.

## Reader problem
Reader hears "sustainable software" and either:
- Dismisses it as green marketing
- Reduces it to "green hosting = done"
- Thinks it's only about carbon and has nothing to do with actual engineering work

## Thesis
Sustainable software is not a badge, a hosting provider, or a separate discipline. It is a quality of engineering decisions — about performance, maintainability, architecture, dependencies, content weight, and longevity. Green hosting helps, but it is one small piece.

## What the reader should be able to do or understand after reading
- See that sustainability connects to things they already care about (speed, cost, maintainability)
- Spot the misconception that a "green hosting" checkbox is enough
- Have a practical framework for what sustainable software actually means in engineering terms
